Privacy policy

In force from 5 November 2026 · iOS and Android apps

Version française

In short

  • No sign-up
  • No ads
  • No tracking
  • Nothing sold

Tult works without signing up. Your players, games and statistics are kept on your phone. A copy of your games is also kept online, so your friends find them in their statistics and you find yours on another phone. Only your profile photo is shown to your friends: your game photos stay on your phone. A live tournament can be seen by anyone who has its code.

Who publishes the app

Tult is published by Maxime Lemaitre, in France, who is responsible for the processing described here. For any question about this policy: jointultapp@gmail.com.

What is kept online

On first launch, the app creates an anonymous account for your phone, without asking for a name or an address. It uses it to keep the following information online:

A game only enters a person’s statistics if whoever recorded it added them with their friend code or QR code, or if they confirmed being one of that phone’s guests. Everyone only sees the games they played in, along with their friends and their profiles. Exchanges with the server are encrypted. Game photos and other players’ photos, solo challenges, settings and backups only stay on your phone.

Service providers

For this online data, Tult uses service providers who act on its behalf and on its instructions:

  • Supabase (Supabase Inc.) hosts the database and manages the accounts.
  • Resend (Resend Inc.) sends the email containing your sign-in code, if you link your account with an email address. It receives your address and that code.
  • Google identifies you if you choose to sign in with Google, under its own privacy policy. Tult then receives your email address.
  • Netlify (Netlify Inc.) hosts the tult.app website, including the live tournament pages. Like any host, it receives the IP address of the visitors of these pages.

These companies are based in the United States. Transfers of data outside the European Union are covered by the European Commission’s standard contractual clauses. No data is sold or used for advertising.

Legal basis

This processing is needed to provide the service you use: count your games, share them with the players concerned and let you find them again (Article 6(1)(b) of the GDPR).

Permissions requested

  • Camera: to scan a QR code and, if you wish, take an avatar photo or photos during and at the end of a game.
  • Photos: to pick an avatar image or a souvenir photo from your gallery.

These permissions are only requested when you use the feature concerned. You can refuse or withdraw them at any time in your phone’s settings; the rest of the app keeps working. The images you pick are copied into the app; they only leave it in a backup you create yourself, except your profile photo, shown to your friends in a reduced version.

What you choose to share

  • QR code, friend code and link: your QR code contains your name, colour, emoji, friend code and account identifier, never your photo. A guest’s contains their name, colour and emoji. The link that lets a guest find their games works only once and expires after 7 days.
  • Results card: when you share an image at the end of a game or tournament, it shows the names, scores and ratings, but not the players’ photos. It goes through your phone’s share menu. The app or service you choose (messaging, social network…) then applies its own privacy policy.
  • Game photos: the photos taken during or at the end of a game stay on your phone. They only leave it if you share the one chosen as the souvenir (with a banner showing the game, the winner and the date), through your phone’s share menu, or in a backup.
  • Backing up your data: from the settings, the app can create a file containing your players and your games (photos included), your tournaments, your challenges and your settings. You choose where to save or send it (Files, iCloud Drive, Google Drive, email…); that service then applies its own privacy policy. The file is not encrypted: keep it somewhere safe. It is only used to restore your data in Tult, on this phone or another one.
  • Review request: after a few games, the app may show the App Store or Google Play rating window. If you leave a rating or a review, it is sent to Apple or Google and published under their terms; the app receives nothing from it.

Live tournaments

With the premium option, a tournament’s organiser can show it on a big screen and let the participants follow it, on tult.app, with the tournament’s code or its QR code. These pages show the tournament’s name, its place if entered, the team and player names, their colours and emojis, the matches, the courts and the scores, never a photo. Anyone who has the code can see them, without an account: only put in names the participants agree to see on display.

On the participants’ page, the team you follow is remembered on your phone, or in your browser on the web. In the app, if you say which of the players you are, the tournament counts in your statistics once it is over.

A live tournament is no longer shown 30 days after its last change. It is erased when the organiser deletes it or deletes their account.

Tips

From the settings, you can leave a tip. The payment is handled entirely by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play), under their terms and privacy policy: the app sees neither your name, nor your account, nor your payment details. It only remembers, on your phone, the date of your last tip, to thank you. A tip unlocks no feature and is not a subscription.

Voice announcements

If you turn on voice announcements, the scores are read by the text-to-speech engine built into your phone (iOS or Android). The app does not use the microphone and records no sound.

Other players

If you add people close to you as players, especially with their photo, make sure they agree. Their names, colours and emojis are kept online with your games, and are only visible to the people who played those games, except in a live tournament, which anyone with its code can see. Their photos stay on your phone.

How long data is kept, and deleting it

On your phone, data stays until you delete it. You can delete a game or a tournament from its screen, or by swiping it to the left in the lists. A removed player no longer shows in the lists but stays linked to their past games. To erase everything from the phone, use « Erase all data » in the settings, or uninstall the app.

Online, data is kept as long as your account exists. A game you delete is also deleted for the other players; a friend’s game you remove from your statistics only goes for you. To delete your account, open the settings and choose « Delete my account »: your profile, profile photo, friend code, friends, players and online games are erased, and the games played with people who have an account stay in their statistics, with no link to you. Uninstalling the app does not delete this online data. You can also ask for deletion from this page or by writing to jointultapp@gmail.com.

Your phone’s backups (iCloud or Google) may include the app’s data, depending on your system settings. They then fall under Apple’s or Google’s policy.

Children

The app can be used by the whole family. It asks for no age or real name, and uses no data for advertising. Before linking an account to an email address, a child under 15 needs a parent’s agreement.

Your rights

The General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) gives you a right of access, rectification, erasure, portability and objection. You can view, change, export and delete your information directly in the app, and delete your account in the settings. For any other request, write to jointultapp@gmail.com: you will get an answer within one month. You can also lodge a complaint with the CNIL, the French data protection authority.

Changes

This policy is updated when the app changes. The current version is always published on this page, with the date it comes into force.
